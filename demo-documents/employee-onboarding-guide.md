# Acme SaaS — Employee Onboarding Guide

## Onboarding Steps for New Employees
New hires should complete the following onboarding steps within their first week:

1. **Sign employment documents** — Complete and return the signed offer letter, NDA,
   and tax forms to HR on day one.
2. **IT account setup** — Collect your laptop from IT, set up your company email, and
   enable two-factor authentication on all accounts.
3. **Tooling access** — Request access to Slack, the project tracker, the code repository,
   and the shared drive through the internal access portal.
4. **Team introduction** — Attend the team welcome meeting and schedule 1:1s with your
   manager and immediate teammates.
5. **Training modules** — Complete the mandatory security awareness and product overview
   training in the learning portal.
6. **30-day check-in** — Schedule a 30-day review with your manager to discuss progress
   and goals.

## Buddy Program
Each new employee is assigned an onboarding buddy for their first 30 days to answer
day-to-day questions and help them settle in.

## Equipment
Standard equipment includes a laptop, monitor, keyboard, and mouse. Additional equipment
requests must be approved by the team manager.
