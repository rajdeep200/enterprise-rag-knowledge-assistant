# Acme SaaS — HR Leave Policy

## 1. Annual Leave
Every full-time employee is entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave accrues at 2 days per completed month of service.

## 2. Approval Requirements
- Leave of 1 to 3 consecutive days may be approved by the employee's direct manager.
- Leave of 4 or more consecutive days requires approval from both the direct manager
  and the department head. Employees taking more than 3 consecutive days must submit the
  request at least 7 days in advance.
- Leave longer than 10 consecutive days additionally requires HR sign-off.

## 3. Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required
for any sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.

## 4. Carry Forward
A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days may be carried forward into the next calendar year.
Any balance above 5 days is forfeited on 31 December.

## 5. Public Holidays
Public holidays are not counted against annual leave. If a public holiday falls during an
approved leave period, that day is not deducted from the employee's leave balance.
